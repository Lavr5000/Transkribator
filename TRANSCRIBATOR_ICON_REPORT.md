## 🎨 Design Philosophy: Sonic Resonance

Created using **canvas-design** skill - museum-quality visual craftsmanship.

### Visual Language

**Sonic Resonance** treats sound as tangible geometry—where the invisible architecture of voice becomes form.

#### Core Elements:
- **Gradient:** Navy dark (#1a1a2e) → Electric cyan (#00d4ff)
  - Maps the journey from internal thought to external speech
  - Each color shift represents a frequency made visible

- **Microphone Geometry:** Minimalist crystallization
  - Perfect circle + single vertical line
  - Reduced to essential formal gesture
  - Countless hours of refinement

- **Sound Waves:** Concentric arcs
  - Mathematical spacing progression
  - Opacity calibrated for depth
  - Visual representation of acoustic propagation

- **"T" Letter:** Integrated into composition
  - Represents "Transkribator" and "Text"
  - Shadow for depth
  - Inevitable positioning through meticulous adjustment

- **Squircle Form:** Rounded square
  - Meeting point of organic (voice) and systematic (AI)
  - Modern app icon convention
  - Radius obsessively refined
